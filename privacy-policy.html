2. Information We Collect and Why

A. Information You Provide (On-Device Storage)

To provide its core functionality (connecting to Minecraft servers), the app stores the following data exclusively on your device:

Data Where Stored Purpose
Offline character names (usernames) SharedPreferences (local storage) Identification on Minecraft servers in offline mode
Saved server list (address, port, label, version, auto-commands) SharedPreferences (local storage) Quick access to your favourite servers
Microsoft account profile (Minecraft username, UUID) SharedPreferences (local storage) Premium server authentication

None of the above data is transmitted to any server operated by us — we do not operate one. We do not collect or store chat messages on our side.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — this data is necessary for the app to function.

B. Automatically Collected Data (Third-Party SDKs)

The app uses third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for stability, monetisation, and subscription management. These SDKs may collect the data described below:

  1. Firebase Crashlytics (Google): Used exclusively for automatic, anonymous crash reporting. Reports contain the device type, OS version, and stack traces, but do not contain any personal data (usernames, chat messages, or server addresses). Collection is disabled in debug builds (setCrashlyticsCollectionEnabled(false)) and is active only in release builds.
  2. Google AdMob: The free version of MineChat displays banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may process the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and IP address for ad serving and fraud prevention, under Google's own Privacy Policy. MineChat Pro subscribers see no ads, and the AdMob SDK does not request further data for them.
  3. RevenueCat: Used to manage in-app subscriptions (MineChat Pro) and entitlement checking. RevenueCat tracks an anonymous "App User ID" and purchase history, but has no access to your credit card details — payments are processed solely by Google Play Billing. See: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Legal basis:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalised advertising (see Section 3).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for crash reporting and app stability (Crashlytics), subscription management (RevenueCat).

5. Android Auto Backup

The app supports Android Auto Backup, which preserves your saved characters, servers, and settings across reinstalls. The following data is excluded from backup for security and practical reasons:

  • Microsoft refresh token (FlutterSecureStorage.xml) — the Android Keystore encryption key is non-exportable; restoring the ciphertext on a different device would produce an undecryptable blob and a broken login state.
  • Map tile data (map_data/) and log files (logs/) — their unbounded growth could exceed the 25 MB Auto Backup quota, silently disabling backup for the useful data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data: Minecraft credentials, settings, servers, and characters remain on your device as long as the app is installed. Signing out, deleting characters/servers, or uninstalling the app / clearing its data removes all locally stored information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash report data is retained by Google for approximately 90 days, per their retention policy.
  • AdMob: Data collected by AdMob is typically deleted or anonymised within 9 to 18 months, depending on Google's specific policies.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription history is retained on the RevenueCat platform for the duration of the subscription and account validity.

How to Delete Your Data

  • On your device, go to Settings → Apps → MineChat → Storage → Clear Data, then uninstall the app.
  • If you used a Microsoft account, tap Sign Out within the app first — this deletes the Microsoft refresh token from the secure storage.
